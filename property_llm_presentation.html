            INTERVIEW PITCH GUIDE • NEXUS OMNIRISK AI ARCHITECTURE
          
          
How to Walk the Interviewer Through OmniRisk AI

        

        
      

      

        

          
1. Legacy Bottleneck vs. Autonomous OmniRisk AI

          
"When a homeowner requests a property insurance valuation, they upload 10–15 photos covering every interior room and exterior elevation. Previously, human underwriters manually tagged photos across two tiers: L1 primary zones (rooftop, bathroom, balcony, interior, exterior) and L2 granular details (floor material, roof shingle class, construction framing, patio cover presence, window glazing). We replaced this 20-minute manual bottleneck with an autonomous Cosmos VLM + Web Agent RAG pipeline that generates quotes in under 200ms."

        

        

          
2. Geodesic EXIF GPS & Ownership Fraud Gate (Stage 1)

          
"To guarantee the uploaded photos actually belong to the insured house, our FastAPI + AsyncIO ingestion gate extracts camera EXIF GPS coordinates, computes the Haversine geodesic distance against the County GIS parcel centroid (≤50m tolerance), checks C2PA/SHA-256 hashes against Photoshop manipulation, and fuzzy-matches the applicant's name against the County Tax Assessor APN warranty deed."

        

        

          
3. Why NVIDIA Cosmos Beat Qwen & Nemotron + QLoRA Fine-Tuning (Stages 2 & 4)

          
"We benchmarked Qwen2.5-VL, NVIDIA Nemotron-VL, and NVIDIA Cosmos. Cosmos led with 98.0% L1 accuracy and superior 3D spatial grounding. For granular L2 property attributes, we fine-tuned Cosmos using QLoRA (Rank r=64) on our proprietary dataset of labeled underwriting images, lifting unstructured classification accuracy from a 78% baseline to 91.5%+ in production."

        

        

          
4. Autonomous Web Crawl Agents for Unclear Images & RAGAS Governance (Stages 3 & 5)

          
"Customer photos are often backlit or occluded—you cannot reliably tell from pixels alone if glass is dual-pane hurricane impact or if a shaded backyard has a permitted patio cover. Our LangGraph + Playwright web agents take the property address, crawl Redfin, Flutter Homes, Zillow, and County Building Permits, and inject those extracted facts into Cosmos via RAG—slashing manual underwriter lookup time by 85% while sustaining 0.92 Faithfulness and 0.89 Context Precision in RAGAS."
