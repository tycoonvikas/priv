How to Present the 6-Stage Algorithmic Trading System

1. Problem Statement & Institutional Goal

"When an institutional desk needs to buy 150,000 shares of a liquid tech stock like NVDA, dumping the order into the lit market causes severe price slippage and alerts high-frequency predatory traders. Standard VWAP/TWAP slices blindly on a fixed schedule. We built a 6-stage multi-agent execution system that adapts in real time—delivering a 14.2 BPS reduction in slippage and outperforming VWAP by 22% and TWAP by 18% across 1.5M+ backtested trades."

2. Stage 1 & Stage 2: GMM Regime Clustering + 30-Second Price/Liquidity Predictor

"First, we combine unsupervised Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) and Hidden Markov Models (HMM) on Level-2 Limit Order Book microstructure features (Order Book Imbalance, Kyle's Lambda, spread variance) with a LangChain macro/news ingestion pipeline that cuts regime-shift detection latency by 450ms. Second, conditioned on the active regime cluster, our forecasting model predicts the next 30 seconds of micro-price drift and L2 order book depth with 96% tick-level accuracy."

3. Stage 3 & Stage 4: Fill Probability / Market Impact → PPO Reinforcement Learning Brain

"Third, our impact simulator uses an Almgren-Chriss + Hawkes Process formulation to estimate both fill probability $P(\text{Fill} \mid \Delta t \le 30\text{s})$ and how our own order size will move the market (temporary + permanent impact in BPS). Fourth, our PPO Actor-Critic Reinforcement Learning Brain ingests the GMM cluster, 30s price/liquidity forecast, and impact curve to maximize a BPS reward function—choosing the optimal slice size and aggressiveness."

4. Stage 5 & Stage 6: Smart Venue Router (NYSE, NASDAQ, Dark Pools, IEX)

"Finally, the child order is dispatched to our Smart Venue Router, which splits liquidity across NASDAQ (capturing maker rebates), NYSE ARCA, Institutional Dark Pools (Sigma X2 / UBS ATS) with Minimum Acceptable Quantity (MAQ) anti-gaming constraints, and IEX (350μs speed-bump protection)."
